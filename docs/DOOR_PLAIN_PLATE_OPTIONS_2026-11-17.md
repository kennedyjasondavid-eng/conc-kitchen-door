# Optional plain plates in Edit Menu

This feature adds choices for supervisors; it does not replace the current rotation or change existing resident assignments. Current sheets remain the baseline. Breakfast activation stays paused.

## Choose a plate

Open a lunch or dinner in **Menu Config → Edit Menu**. The existing plain/soft alternatives and restriction-specific fields still accept your own descriptions. Each now also offers **Choose a plain plate…**, initially blank.

| Protein | Starch choices | Vegetable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain Chicken Leg | Plain Rice (No Added Salt), Plain Roasted Yams, or no starch | Plain Carrot and Zucchini |
| Plain Basa | Same three choices | Plain Carrot and Zucchini |
| Plain Tofu | Same three choices | Plain Carrot and Zucchini |

The nine combinations use six existing CODEX component recipes. Each has ingredients and preparation instructions there; DOOR does not keep a separate ingredient catalogue. Chicken and fish are animal proteins; basa contains fish and tofu contains soy. Carrot/zucchini is distinct from the existing stir-fry recipe, which includes green beans and bell peppers. No-starch choices omit the starch component; they do not rewrite a resident's recorded portion or protein instruction.

Choose the destination first by using its field: plain/soft, Gluten Free, Diabetic/Low Carb, No Dairy, No Egg/Mustard, Bland/No Spicy or Anaphylactic. A field follows its existing routing rules. Selecting a plate does not create a new restriction, assign a resident by room number, override another requirement or clear anaphylactic containment.

## Check the draft outcome

The editor shows the routed residents, recipe declarations, detected conflicts and recorded preparation instructions. A controlling plain/soft row also identifies non-processed recipients when the existing non-processed plate uses that row. Multiple plain/soft rows keep their existing selection rules; the new options do not create an arbitrary rule for each row.

For example, a basa choice for a GF group containing someone with No Fish is flagged. Saving that newly chosen incompatible option is blocked, and the draft remains available to correct. Recipe facts are checked again at save; missing or ambiguous canonical entries cannot be selected. Restoring the original value does not introduce a new gate on the old meal. Existing manual text paths and untouched saved definitions retain their current behavior.

**Save Edit** applies a deliberate change through the normal menu workflow. Opening the editor, viewing options or cancelling changes no meal or sheet. The complete plate names survive save, reopen, publication and another-device replay. Exact new roster selections contribute their actual component counts to the existing HOUSE routing feed, including distinct plain/soft choices; main-meal sides are not appended to them. Their printed declarations use current recipe facts, or explicitly say unverified if those facts are unavailable. Existing descriptions keep their existing behavior.

## What the options do not certify

Check the resident instruction, actual ingredient package and preparation before serving. A plain recipe name, no-added-salt recipe or field label is not a product allergen guarantee, nutritional prescription, halal procurement confirmation or approved clinical texture. Chicken-leg preparation for someone needing soft food follows the recorded instruction and CODEX preparation, including removal of bones where directed. Portion quantities are not invented.

No current menu/registry snapshots, breakfast jobs, production schedules or consumer schemas are changed by this feature. The existing manual-comparison findings remain separate work; adding options does not declare that reconciliation complete.
